XJJ is a video player, not a video host. It streams content from sources you or the site operator configure: local playlist files (videos.txt / videos.json) and/or third-party community API endpoints that return video URLs. The application itself does not store, produce, upload, or pre-screen any video files.

We do not control, and make no representations about, the accuracy, legality, or appropriateness of content returned by third-party sources. Content may change or disappear at any time and may be unpredictable.

If you are a copyright holder (or an authorized agent) and believe content played through this site infringes your copyright, you may submit a takedown notice. To help us act quickly, please include:

  • The specific source/API name and video URL (or, for API sources, the approximate time and source you saw it on) shown in the app;

Upon receiving a valid notice, we will disable or remove the reported source/URL from rotation.
